Key findings — what the store data confirms
Bellville Mega (−25.3%): Groceries alone lost R12,402 — 65% of Bellville's total decline. Basket dropped R547 → R463. Likely a local competitive threat.
Paarl Mega (−12.0%): Groceries + Household + Apparel all declining. Broader discretionary pullback. Basket R523 → R464.
Tygervalley (−6.7%): Visits flat, basket shrinking — classic price-sensitivity pattern. Electronics growing (+21.9%) while staples fall.
Bright spots: Khayelitsha (+29.6%), Mitchell's Plain (+39.7%), Sea Point (+31.7%) prove no brand problem — targeted stores can grow fast.
Board answer: Three actions — audit the three failing Mega stores immediately, re-engage Silver-tier customers with a targeted offer, and investigate competitive threats near Bellville and Paarl. The Pap Power Promo model (+50.5% volume in April 2025) is the template for the retention campaign.
Immediate — 0 to 4 weeks
Operational audit: Bellville, Paarl & Tygervalley Mega
Three Mega stores account for R30,819 in lost revenue — 132% of the total WC gap. Conduct store-level audit. Focus on Groceries and Household shelf availability, pricing, and competitor proximity.

Bellville is the priority: −R18,827 alone. February 2025 saw a 45% basket crash that has only partially recovered.
Target: audit within 3 weeks
Short-term — 1 to 3 months
Retain Silver-tier WC loyalty customers
Silver members are visiting 10.3% less often and spending less per trip. Gold is stable. Silver re-engagement is the priority action before more churn occurs.

Run a targeted Groceries/Household offer for WC Silver members only. Model the structure on the Pap Power Promo (+50.5% volume in April 2025). Consider a 20% discount rather than 33% to protect margin while restoring visit frequency.
Template: Pap Power Promo mechanic
Investigate — ongoing
Identify and respond to competitive threat
The Bellville Groceries pattern — high volume, fast decline, concentrated in staples — is the signature of a new competitor opening nearby. Tygervalley's flat visits + falling baskets is consistent with price competition.

Check for store openings near Bellville and Paarl. Survey Silver-tier WC members who have not transacted in 90+ days. Replicate the Khayelitsha growth playbook in affected catchments.
Focus: Bellville & Paarl catchments
